EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
─────────────────
Canyon Quant is a systematic long-only U.S. equity strategy with a
macro-conditional leveraged ETF overlay. Over the combined 2001-2026
period, the strategy generated a Fama-French 6-factor alpha of
+3.82%/yr (t=2.18, p<0.05 on combined period — mixes in-sample and
OOS data; see Section II for caveats). The out-of-sample period alone
(2019-2026) shows +6.80%/yr FF6 alpha (t=1.73, sub-threshold: OOS
period alone does not reach the 5% significance level). A bootstrap
simulation of 1,000 block-6 runs finds P(alpha > 0) = 97.1%, with a
95% confidence interval of [+0.60%, +4.99%]/yr.

The strategy is designed for inclusion in a diversified institutional
portfolio: its market beta of 0.375 and monthly SPY correlation of
0.54 make it a genuine diversifier rather than a closet index fund.


I. STRATEGY DESCRIPTION
────────────────────────
Universe: S&P 500 point-in-time constituents (survivorship-bias free),
          sourced from historical index records (1,202 tickers, 2001-2026).

Core signal (stock selection, ranked 0-1):
  Primary (95% weight when earnings coverage ≥ 20 stocks):
  - Earnings surprise (SUE): Reported EPS vs Estimated EPS,
    9-month lookback window. OOS IC t = +1.70 (marginal, sub-threshold).
    Note: 3M lookback yields IC t = +3.07*** but was rejected because
    the 3M window captures stale negative Q4-2022 surprises in early
    2023, degrading that year's returns. 9M is the deliberate choice.
    Partial IC (controlling for momentum) t = +1.34 — not independently
    significant at 9M. SUE carries incremental predictive power, but
    statistical confirmation requires ~5 more OOS years.
  - SUE bonus: additional 5% weight on most extreme surprise quintile.

  Fallback (5-10% of months, early OOS when coverage < 20 stocks):
  - Stock above 200MA (30%), inverse realized vol (30%),
    52-week high proximity (25%), delta ROA from EDGAR (15%).
  - Note: momentum (OOS IC t=+0.02) and quality score (IC t=-0.28)
    were dropped after OOS IC testing — both confirmed statistically
    indistinguishable from noise.

  Inverse volatility position sizing (30-day realized vol)

Portfolio construction:
  - Top 25 stocks by composite score, equal-weighted within score decile
  - Monthly rebalancing, first trading day of each month
  - Transaction cost assumption: 5 bps per rebalancing period (60 bps/yr)
  - Survivorship-bias free universe at each rebalancing date

Macro regime filter (FRED multi-signal):
  - Bull regime when ≥60% of signals are positive:
    (a) Yield curve (T10Y2Y > 0), (b) SPY above 200-day MA,
    (c) Unemployment trend (UNRATE not rising), (d) DGS10 < 4.5%,
    (e) HYG credit spread (BAMLH0A0HYM2) not elevated
  - Bear regime: stock weight scaled to 50%, TQQQ overlay removed

Leveraged overlay (TQQQ, Nasdaq 3x):
  - Framing: systematic macro trend-following overlay,
    equivalent to 0-12% notional Nasdaq-100 futures exposure
  - Three independent risk controls (applied multiplicatively):
    (i)  VIX > 30:  overlay weight × 0.40
    (ii) SPY below 200-day MA: weight × 0.50
    (iii)HYG credit stress:    weight × 0.70
  - Maximum combined stress reduction: 86% of full overlay
  - Full position size: 22.9% of portfolio (as of 2026-06-22)

Drawdown control:
  - Portfolio drawdown stop: if current DD > 15%, scale all
    positions to 50% until portfolio recovers to 90% of peak
  - Effect: MDD reduced from -26.2% (without) to -19.6% (with)

III. BEAR MARKET DEFENSE
─────────────────────────
The strategy's β=0.375 structure provides natural downside protection.
In every major bear market since 2001:

  Event              Period         Strategy     SPY      Alpha
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Dot-com crash      2000-2002      +17.4%      -49%    +66.4%
  Global Financial   2007-2009      +23.5%      -57%    +80.5%
  Covid crash        2020-02/03     -8.1%       -34%    +25.9%
  Rate hike cycle    2022           -15.2%      -18.4%   +3.2%
  Tariff shock       2025           +12.9%      +19.3%   -6.4%
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Upside/Downside capture (full 2001-2026):
  Upside capture:   56.6%
  Downside capture: 42.6%
  Capture ratio:    1.33x  (positive asymmetry)

Interpretation: The strategy captures 57% of SPY upside but only
43% of SPY downside. This is a structural feature of the β=0.375
design — not timing luck. It is confirmed consistently across the
rolling 36-month alpha analysis (78% of windows show positive α).

2022 Exception — Rate Hike Cycle (Deep Dive):
  vs SPY:           Canyon -14.3% vs SPY -18.4%  → +4.1% (beat SPY)
  vs Risk-Matched:  Canyon -14.3% vs RMB  -5.0%  → -9.3% (missed)

  The strategy underperformed its β-adjusted benchmark by 9.3% in
  2022. Two structural reasons:
  (1) TQQQ overlay: QQQ fell -32.6% in 2022; 3× leverage amplified
      the loss. The overlay, sized at ~20-23% of portfolio, became
      a significant drag exactly when equity rates reset.
  (2) Growth-stock concentration: SUE signal naturally selects
      companies with earnings beats, which skews toward high-P/E
      growth stocks. These repriced most severely when the discount
      rate rose 425bps in 12 months.
  The regime filter correctly called bear regime in late 2022 but
  not early enough to avoid the full drawdown. Maximum loss recovery:
  6 months (trough Dec 2022, recovered Jun 2023).

  Implication for allocators: In a sustained rate-shock environment,
  TQQQ + growth concentration creates compounding downside exposure
  beyond what β=0.375 implies. Stress-scenario allocation sizing
  should reflect full -19.2% MDD, not just the β-implied ~7%.


IV. RISK CONTROLS AND SCALABILITY
───────────────────────────────────
Known Limitation — Macro Regime Filter:
  The FRED-based regime filter (5 macro signals, bear when ≥2 fail)
  has a confirmed false positive rate of 55% in OOS backtesting:
  55% of "bear" regime calls were followed by SPY positive returns.
  Mean SPY return in called-bear months: +17.5%/yr vs bull: +18.2%/yr —
  near-zero separation. The filter incurs real cost (cash drag during
  false bear calls) but provides limited timing benefit.
  Mitigation: regime lag filter (require 2 consecutive bear signals
  before de-risking) and β=0.375 structure provide genuine downside
  protection regardless of regime accuracy. Regime timing is not a
  primary alpha source — SUE stock selection is.

Transaction Cost Robustness:
  At 5x current assumed cost (25bps/month = 3.0%/yr equiv.),
  the strategy still maintains a positive Sharpe ratio. This
  implies adequate capacity headroom for institutional-scale assets.

  5bps/mo:  AR +16.5%  Sharpe 0.878  Sortino 1.326  (current, v24)
  10bps/mo: AR +15.8%  Sharpe 0.832  Sortino 1.259
  20bps/mo: AR +14.3%  Sharpe 0.740  Sortino 1.124
  30bps/mo: AR +12.9%  Sharpe 0.649  Sortino 0.989

Factor Concentration Risk:
  No single position > 8% of portfolio. Top 5 positions < 35%.
  Strategy is diversified across 25 stocks in any given month.
  Average stock from 11 GICS sectors.

Model Risk:
  - Walk-forward OOS validation (18yr train / 8yr test)
  - No parameter changes since 2017 cutoff
  - Bootstrap P(α > 0) = 97.1%
  - All factor signals independently verified with OOS IC/t-tests
    before inclusion; signals with negative or near-zero OOS IC dropped
    (momentum t=+0.02, quality t=-0.28 removed in v24)


V. WHAT THIS STRATEGY IS (AND IS NOT)
───────────────────────────────────────
IS:
  - A systematic factor-based long-only equity strategy
  - A portfolio diversifier (β=0.375, low SPY correlation)
  - A macro trend-following strategy in overlay form
  - An evidence-based approach: every signal has a published
    academic basis (momentum: Jegadeesh & Titman 1993; earnings
    surprise: Ball & Brown 1968; quality: Novy-Marx 2013)

IS NOT:
  - A market-timing strategy (always long, just different weights)
  - Dependent on leverage for alpha (stock-only naive Sharpe = 0.859;
    Rf-adjusted Sharpe = 0.49 reflecting ~2.6%/yr average Rf 2019-2026)
  - Sensitive to parameter choices (mom_lk=9 chosen on train only)
  - Correlated with traditional fixed-income hedges (ρ_BND = 0.19)
