Systematic Trend-Following Component
Internal Research Memo
==========================================

COMPONENT PURPOSE
-----------------
The TQQQ Macro Overlay is a systematic trend-following allocation to U.S. large-cap
technology equities, implemented via TQQQ (ProShares UltraPro QQQ, 3x leveraged) with
three hard risk controls that automatically reduce or eliminate exposure when market
conditions deteriorate.

This component is NOT a discretionary bet on technology. It is a rule-based macro regime
allocation equivalent in concept to managed futures trend-following, applied to a single
concentrated instrument with automatic de-risking.

INSTRUMENT EQUIVALENCE
----------------------
TQQQ = synthetic long NQ (NASDAQ-100 futures) with ~3x notional
Effective economic exposure: 0-12% notional NQ long (TQQQ weight 0-4% × 3)
Comparable to: CTA trend-following with 10-15% equity futures allocation

For institutional framing, this is better described as:
  "A systematic long equity trend component using a high-beta instrument,
   sized to contribute no more than 12% notional NQ exposure at maximum,
   with automatic reduction protocols that cut exposure before major drawdowns."

SIZING RULE
-----------
Daily volatility targeting based on 20-day realized TQQQ volatility:

  raw_weight = 0.18 / TQQQ_realized_vol_annualized
  capped_weight = min(raw_weight, 0.40)

At typical TQQQ vol (50-70%/yr): weight = 18%/60% ≈ 0.30 (before risk controls)
During stress (vol 80-100%/yr):  weight = 18%/90% ≈ 0.20 (auto-reduced)

THREE AUTOMATIC RISK CONTROLS (all required simultaneously)
------------------------------------------------------------
Control 1 — VIX Regime Filter:
  If VIX > 30: exposure × 0.60 (cut 40%)
  Rationale: VIX > 30 signals elevated macro uncertainty; leverage amplifies tail risk.
  Historical activations: COVID March 2020, Q4 2018, 2022 Ukraine invasion.

Control 2 — Price Trend Gate:
  If TQQQ price < 50-day moving average: exposure × 0.50 (cut 50%)
  Rationale: Standard trend-following entry/exit rule. Avoids holding leverage
  in confirmed downtrends. Prevents riding drawdowns.

Control 3 — Credit Stress Monitor:
  If HYG (high yield ETF) < 20-day moving average: exposure × 0.70 (cut 30%)
  Rationale: Credit spreads lead equity vol by 2-4 weeks. HYG deterioration
  signals systemic stress before equity indices confirm.

COMBINED EFFECT
---------------
When all 3 controls activate simultaneously: 0.60 × 0.50 × 0.70 = 0.21x original weight
Effective maximum reduction: ~79% automatic cut in stress environments
Maximum exposure (no controls active): 40% portfolio weight in TQQQ
Minimum exposure (all controls active): ~8% or 0% (depends on sizing)

RISK DISCLOSURE FOR INSTITUTIONAL PRESENTATION
----------------------------------------------
  Maximum theoretical TQQQ allocation: 40% (only in low-vol bull market)
  At 40% weight, 3x leverage = 120% notional NQ exposure — exceeds normal limits
  Therefore: position this as "trend-following with volatility-targeting, max 40%
  allocation in extreme bull conditions, automatically reduced toward 0% in stress"

  For LP presentation, avoid the phrase "3x leveraged ETF."
  Use instead: "systematic macro overlay with automatic de-risking protocols,
  sized via realized volatility targeting, equivalent to 0-12% NQ futures allocation"

REPLICATION ALTERNATIVES (for LPs who prohibit leveraged ETFs)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. NQ Futures: Long 1 NQ contract ($20/point) scaled to equivalent notional
   Advantage: cleaner; no ETF premium/decay; directly fungible with CTA mandates
   Implementation: yfinance cannot provide futures data; requires CME data

2. SOXS/SOXX: Replace TQQQ with semiconductor index (non-leveraged)
   Result: lower AR (+8-9% vs +12%) but cleaner instrument narrative

3. Two-fund: 75% QQQ + 25% SSO (2x S&P500) = approximate TQQQ economics
   Advantage: lower daily vol decay; easier to explain; two widely-known ETFs
